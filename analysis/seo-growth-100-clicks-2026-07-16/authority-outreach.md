# Authority and link acquisition plan

The 100-click/day target cannot be reached by technical cleanup alone. The calculator and second-opinion checklist are the linkable assets; the vehicle pages are the search landing pages they should strengthen.

## Prospect groups

1. Independent repair shops that publish consumer education.
2. Automotive journalists and used-car newsletters covering ownership cost.
3. Personal-finance writers covering car affordability and sunk costs.
4. Consumer advocates, lemon-law information sites, and maintenance communities.
5. Model-specific owner clubs and forums where the cited diagnosis checklist is directly relevant.
6. Driving schools, credit unions, and employee-benefit resource libraries.

## Outreach rule

Pitch only when a page is genuinely useful to the recipient's audience. Do not mass-send, buy sitewide links, exchange exact-match anchor text, or present an internal estimate as an official failure rate.

## Short pitch: calculator

Subject: Free repair-or-sell cost comparison tool for drivers

We built a free calculator that compares a repair quote and 12-month backlog with the taxes, fees, downtime, and immediate maintenance involved in replacing a car. It is designed as a screening tool, not a substitute for diagnosis. If you maintain a resource page for drivers facing large repair bills, this may be a useful addition: https://automoneypit.com/tools/repair-or-sell-calculator

## Short pitch: second-opinion checklist

Subject: Printable checklist for comparing car repair estimates

We published a concise checklist that helps drivers ask two shops for the same diagnosis evidence, repair scope, itemized costs, and warranty. It includes a copyable request owners can send before a second appointment: https://automoneypit.com/guides/car-repair-estimate-second-opinion

## Monthly cadence

- Week 1: identify 25 highly relevant prospects and record the exact resource-page fit.
- Week 2: send 10-15 personalized pitches and answer replies.
- Week 3: publish one data-backed update or model-specific diagnostic explainer.
- Week 4: review earned links, referral visits, linked pages, and assisted GSC growth.

## Success metrics

- Five qualified referring domains in the first 90 days.
- At least two editorial links to a linkable asset per month after the first cycle.
- Growth in impressions and average position for pages linked from those assets.
- No reliance on paid, automated, or exact-match-anchor link schemes.
